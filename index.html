ABOUT US

Artisanal Heritage of Konaseema

Rooted in the lush green palm groves of Konaseema, Andhra Pradesh, Konaseema CocoCraft was born out of a deep reverence for nature and traditional Indian craftsmanship.

We transform discarded natural coconut shells and wood into elegant, eco-friendly lifestyle crafts, hand-carved rakhis, keychains, and home decor. Every piece empowers local rural artisans while giving eco-conscious individuals a touch of sustainable beauty.
